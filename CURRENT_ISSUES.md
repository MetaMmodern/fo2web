# Current issues

- The website UI still uses NFS branding and names.
- Track ground looks darker than expected.
- The driver is missing from the seat.
- The game displays in 4:3 rather than widescreen.
- During races, HUD elements sometimes disappear or start hidden and appear later.
- After driving for a while, missing HUD and Escape-menu elements appear.
- Opening the pause menu during a race no longer applies the expected dark tint to the background; shadows also appear absent.
- Much of the original game's glow effect is missing, leaving the world looking dull.
- Fade-in and fade-out effects are missing.
- Painted road markings on asphalt are barely visible in sunlight, but more visible in shadow; before the repeated-shadow fix, they were clearer inside the affected area.
